# Acceptance checklist

## Owner visual review
- Three genuinely different Home compositions with comparable content.
- Recognisably Media One; original logo, supplied fonts and real photos.
- Strong hierarchy and obvious first action at phone size.
- No repeated development commentary or meaningless future-module cards.
- Venues distinguishable through imagery and concise useful content.
- No forced cropping through faces, important architecture or venue identity.
- All source-specific labels accurate: suites, office tower, P7 atmosphere.

## Rendered browser checks
Capture 375px, 430px and 1440px widths. Inspect all concepts yourself; record what was fixed. Screenshots are evidence, not a substitute for interaction review.
- No sideways page overflow, clipped text or obstructed controls.
- Confirm downloaded font files are the fonts actually rendered.
- No synthetic bold/italic, tiny body copy or unreadable photo overlays.
- Keyboard navigation/focus, accessible labels, contrast and reduced motion.
- Loading, image failure, empty and error states fit the design.
- Fixed navigation respects safe areas and does not cover content.
- On real iPhone: keyboard/composer, scrolling, back navigation, orientation.
- No unnecessary autoplay, oversized image downloads or layout shifts.

## Integration checks when selected design is adopted
- Staff can edit an offer and eligible content still appears to guests.
- Unauthorised account remains blocked; no cross-venue write access change.
- Knowledge publication, expiry and replacement rules remain effective.
- No reset of database contents or AI allowance.
- No mock booking, request or member balance is presented as real.
- Concierge answer quality is reviewed separately; design does not certify it.

## Stop criteria
Do not declare production ready from passing build/tests. Present unresolved limitations plainly in review notes, not repeated across guest screens. Keep preview private until separately authorised.
